## Intentional adaptations for the requested SVG approach

- 3D zoom/orbit becomes vector composition, scale, parallax and repositioning. The 360° model inspection becomes a readable 2.5D component diagram.
- The worksheet’s placeholder artifact names become Eagle’s descent stage and Spirit, supported by NASA mission references listed in README.
- The worksheet supplies no video. A newly written captioned SVG briefing occupies that story beat; it is identified as original illustration rather than archival footage.
- The future’s dust/wear effects remain an illustrative conservation question. They are not calibrated erosion predictions or actual condition data.
- RISE’s globe cap and round face follow the GLB reference. Expressions are new vector drawings. No limbs or rigged body are invented for the floating round mascot.
- The closing certificate is educational and issued by VantaLumen. It does not imply NASA accreditation.

## Timing and completion

The visitor controls narrative pacing through ordinary scrolling. A scrubbed GSAP playhead aligns the seven scenes to measured chapter offsets. Continuous idle bounce is mild; the future scene uses the smallest amplitude. Scene entry and action feedback each have their own bounce offset. Pausing motion or enabling the system’s reduced-motion setting removes idle/entry motion and presents still poses at chapter changes.

For each world, reveal at least 24 of 40 SVG dust tiles, match the three parts and move the year slider beyond 2026. Then record that world. Recording both worlds unlocks the certificate. Briefing playback is optional; no forced wait blocks the story.
