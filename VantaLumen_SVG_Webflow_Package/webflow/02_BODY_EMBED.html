Meet your storyteller

Greetings, explorer. I am RISE, the Relic Intelligent System & Explorer. My story didn’t start in a high-tech laboratory or a NASA cleanroom. I was originally designed by an 8-year-old boy who dreamed of hugging the stars and understanding the whispers of the cosmos.

Equipped with gravimetric sensors and a heart full of curiosity, I was built to accompany brave astronauts into the dark, feeling every shift of gravity and collecting the untold stories of every spacecraft we left behind. Now, I am here to guide you through humanity’s greatest interplanetary archive. Welcome to VANTALUMEN.
